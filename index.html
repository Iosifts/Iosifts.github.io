About

I am a PhD candidate supervised by Prof. Björn W. Schuller. My doctoral work on audio state and trait modelling began in November 2022 at the Chair of Embedded Intelligence for Health Care and Wellbeing, University of Augsburg, and continues at the Chair of Health Informatics at TUM. I studied Mathematics at the University of Crete, Greece.

Research interests

Affective computing

Emotion recognition from speech, faces and text, and how foundation models change the field.

Speech & audio

Noise-adaptive speech enhancement for hearing aids and paralinguistic analysis of speech.

Foundation models

Probing vision- and audio-language models for shortcuts, biases and interpretable cues.

AI for accessibility & culture

Art descriptions for blind and low-vision audiences and LLM analysis of cultural heritage projects.
