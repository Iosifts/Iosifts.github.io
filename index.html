About

I am a PhD candidate at the Chair of Health Informatics, Technical University of Munich, supervised by Prof. Björn W. Schuller. My doctoral work on audio state and trait modelling began in November 2022 at the Chair of Embedded Intelligence for Health Care and Wellbeing, University of Augsburg. Before that I trained as a mathematician at the University of Crete.

My work spans speech enhancement for hearing aids, affective computing with foundation models, AI for cultural heritage and accessibility, and early fault detection from sensor signals.

Research interests

Affective computing

Emotion recognition from speech, faces and text, and how foundation models are changing the field.

Speech enhancement & hearing aids

Noise- and speaker-adaptive denoising, and bringing multimodal foundation models to hearing aids.

Foundation models

Probing vision- and audio-language models for shortcuts, proxy biases and interpretable acoustic cues.

Computer audition

Acoustic scene classification, zero-shot action recognition from audio, and paralinguistics.

AI for culture & accessibility

LLMs in cultural heritage and art descriptions for blind and low-vision audiences.

Predictive maintenance

Early warning of component failure from remotely sensed signals in vehicle fleets.
